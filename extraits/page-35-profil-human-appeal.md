──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ORGANIZATION MISSION/VISION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Human Appeal (HA) is a British International Non-governmental
Organisation - INGO based in UK founded in 1991 for the purpose
of abolishing poverty by providing humanitarian aid, sustainable
development programs and championing advocacy for our beneficiaries
worldwide. Human Appeal has 7 International Field Offices across
Africa, Asia and the Middle East (Iraq Syria, Pakistan, Palestine,
Somalia, Turkey, and Yemen).

In 2018, Human Appeal has become operational in Palestine and
established its Palestine Field Office to carry out humanitarian
aid and sustainable development projects in multiple sectors,
including: WASH, Health, Protection, Food Security and Livelihoods
and other cross cutting and priority sectors in Palestine such
as climate change.

HA vision: To become the global agent of change for a just,
caring, and sustainable world.

HA mission: We are a global humanitarian and development
organization. We save lives, alleviate poverty, transform and
empower local communities whilst championing humanity,
impartiality, neutrality, and independence.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WASH AREA OF INTERVENTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ⊙ Water                       ⊙ Sanitation and hygiene
  ○ Solid waste management
  ⊙ WASH in institutions        ⊙ Floods mitigation and prevention
  ○ WASH sector governance

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WASH PROGRAM
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Since the establishment of Human Appeal's Field Office in Palestine
in 2018, we have carried out vital Water and Sanitation projects
aiming at improving sustainable access to water and sanitation
services.

Our projects in the sector included the extension of sanitation
household connections, construction of small, medium and largescale
desalination plants, disinfection of all water sources, reconstruction
of stormwater facilities, construction of wastewater networks and
maintenance of critical water wells. Moreover, we have also
distributed COVID-19 tailored hygiene and disinfection kits to
hundreds of households and delivered personal protective equipment
(PPEs) to WASH staff of local municipalities.

Human Appeal has in-house expertise in WASH sector, including our
HA's Senior Global WASH Advisor and holds PhD in Sustainable Water
technologies. We also have experienced technical WASH engineers who
support the implementation of our WASH projects in Gaza and West bank.
Recently, HA has appointed a new Programmes and Operations Manager
in the West Bank who also has advanced university degrees in water
resources management.

Through our internal emergency management structure, HA Palestine has
the capacity to respond to rapid onset emergencies by delivering
immediate short to mid-term interventions.

## Lecture critique

### Ce qui est valide
- Statut officiel de partenaire WASH Cluster reconnu par l'UNICEF
- Adresse Gaza
- Focal point Gaza avec e-mail @humanappeal.org.ps (cohérent avec une véritable présence locale)

### Ce qui est auto-déclaré
- Tout le texte « Mission/vision » et « WASH program »
- Les volumes, ampleurs et nature des projets décrits (« petits, moyens et grande échelle » de désalinisation par exemple)
- L'expertise interne revendiquée

### Ce qui ne figure pas
- Aucun chiffre de bénéficiaires
- Aucun volume d'eau livré
- Aucun budget
- Aucun ranking ou positionnement comparatif
